How many tubes should each difficulty have?

Each tube holds 4 color segments. The extra empty tubes give the player room to maneuver.

A

Classic (4 / 6 / 8 colors)

EASY
4 colors · 6 tubes
MEDIUM
6 colors · 8 tubes · max 40 moves
HARD
8 colors · 10 tubes · max 60 moves
B

Gentle Ramp (3 / 5 / 7 colors)

Slightly easier across the board — good for casual players. Easy has only 3 colors so first-timers can learn the mechanic quickly.

Easy: 3 colors · 5 tubes Medium: 5 colors · 7 tubes · max 35 moves Hard: 7 colors · 9 tubes · max 50 moves
C

Steep Ramp (4 / 7 / 11 colors)

Bigger jump from Medium to Hard — for players who want a real challenge at the top end. Hard becomes very large and complex.

Easy: 4 colors · 6 tubes Medium: 7 colors · 9 tubes · max 55 moves Hard: 11 colors · 13 tubes · max 80 moves
